The bad news: The venerable semantics journal *Natural Language Semantics* founded by Irene Heim and Angelika Kratzer has been discontinued by Springer.

The very good news: The editorial team has started a successor journal [*Semantics of Natural Languages*](https://snl-journal.org), which is a diamond open access journal published by Open Library of Humanities.

The editors have posted [an open letter](https://snl-journal.org/news/942/).

The [first accepted articles](https://snl-journal.org/news/944/) are available as pre-production versions.

This is very exciting! Semantics now has three (!) fully open access journals:

- [*Semantics and Pragmatics*](https://semprag.org)
- [*Meaning: a journal of linguistics and philosophy*](https://www.meaning-journal.org)
- [*Semantics of Natural Languages*](https://snl-journal.org)

You know what to do: support these community-led projects in whatever way you can.
